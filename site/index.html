How it works

Three branches are fused: CNN-BiLSTM with multi-head attention, a quantum-inspired matrix-product-state tensor network, and a 3-qubit variational quantum circuit. The model predicts the 10-minute change in wind speed and adds it to the current reading.

Weights were trained in PyTorch, exported to JSON and re-implemented in plain JavaScript, including a small statevector simulator for the quantum circuit. A test checks that the JS and Python predictions match within 1e-4.
